01  /  The problem

Your stack was built a decade ago.

Campaign execution tools in a retention era.

Every major D2C brand in India runs WebEngage, MoEngage, or CleverTap. These platforms were built as campaign execution tools. They hand you a journey builder and wish you luck.

The result is predictable. Bloated flows nobody updates. Segmentation that needs a data analyst. Campaigns that go stale in ninety days. A retention team that spends seventy percent of its time maintaining infrastructure, not thinking about strategy.

They charge by MAU and event volume. They have no reason to get smarter on your behalf.

02  /  What we do

Retention, run as a revenue function.

We work with your existing data. We identify the highest-value retention opportunities specific to your category, your cohorts, your margin structure. We run them.

Your team sets the goals and the guardrails. Consider the rest on autopilot.

Not a campaign tool with AI bolted on. A platform built, from first principles, for how retention actually compounds.

  • No new dashboards to learn.

  • No engineering tickets.

03  /  Why now

The CAC playbook is finished.

Paid acquisition costs across Indian D2C have roughly doubled in three years. Meta and Google inventory is saturated. The playbook that built the last decade of Indian D2C has run its course. Retention is the only lever left that compounds.

But the tools that power retention today were designed for a world where attention was cheap and first-party data was thin. That world is over. The brands that rebuild retention in the next eighteen months will own the next decade.

18 mo. The window in which category leadership gets re-drawn.
